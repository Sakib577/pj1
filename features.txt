FEATURES

STATUS LEGEND
✅ = Fully implemented and verified in the source code
⚠️ = Partially implemented (the missing part is listed in "MISSING / PARTIAL")

CORE FINANCE
- ✅ Transactions: add/edit/delete income & expense entries with categories, notes, and timestamps.
- ✅ Categories: custom income/expense categories with icons, colors, and subcategories.
- ✅ Budgets: per-category and rolling-period budgets with progress and overspend alerts.
- ⚠️ Savings Goals: target amounts and contribution-linked progress. Deadlines are NOT implemented.
- ⚠️ Debts: track borrowed/lent amounts with settlement status. Due dates are NOT implemented.
- ✅ Planned Payments: scheduled payments with local notification reminders.
- ⚠️ Shopping Lists: categorized lists with purchase tracking. Cost estimates are NOT implemented.

ANALYTICS & REPORTS
- ✅ Statistics Dashboard: trend lines, income vs expense, cash flow with net line, savings trend,
  balance history, category donut, spending patterns, top expenses, budget progress,
  cash-flow forecast, and next-month estimates.
- ✅ Reports: Cash Flow Statement, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Budget vs Actual, Debt,
  Savings & Net Worth for a selectable period.
- ✅ Export: PDF and CSV export of reports and transaction history via the share sheet.

TOOLS & PLATFORM
- ✅ Currency Converter: real-time exchange-rate conversion.
- ✅ Multi-Currency: selectable display currency with live exchange rates.

SECURITY & UX
- ✅ Firebase Auth with Google Sign-In and email verification.
- ✅ Biometric/PIN app lock.
- ✅ Home-screen widget with balance preview.
- ✅ Notification history and settings.
- ✅ Offline-first support via Firestore offline persistence.
- ✅ Crash error screen with persisted crash log.

MISSING / PARTIAL (from the original list, not implemented in code)
- ⚠️ Savings Goals: "deadlines" — there is no target-date/deadline field on the savings goal model or page.
- ⚠️ Debts: "due dates" — the debt record has no due-date field (only borrowed/lent, settlement, partial repayments).
- ⚠️ Shopping Lists: "cost estimates" — items only store a price when completed; there is no pre-set estimated cost.
